# Unicode
Decomposed: Café, niño, Ångström, Zürich.

A value with decomposed letters: Zoë Nüñez.

Zero-width characters render as nothing: zerowidthjoinwordjoinend.

No-break space and narrow stay: a b.

Typographic: “curly quotes”, ‘single’, en–dash, em—dash, ellipsis…, © ® ™ € £ ¥ ± × ÷ ½ ° § ¶ •.

Latin Extended and others: Łódź, Győr, Çelik, Ñandú, ΑΒΓ αβγ, АБВ абв.

Ligature words: office financial benefit affluent waffle fluff.
