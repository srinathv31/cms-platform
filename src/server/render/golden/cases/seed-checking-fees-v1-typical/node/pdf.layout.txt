## Offer details
Hi Maya, here are the fees for your Everyday Checking account.

 • The monthly service fee is waived when your daily balance stays at or above $1,000.

 • Your first set of checks is free.

 • Online and mobile banking are always free.

## Rates and fees

 Fee | Amount

 Monthly service fee | $12

 Out-of-network ATM fee | $2.50

 Stop payment | $25

 Overdraft fee | $0

 Paper statement | $3 per month

## Legal notices
Deposits are insured by the FDIC up to $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each
ownership category.

     We may change fees with 30 days' written notice.

Account terms for New Jersey residents are in your deposit account agreement.
